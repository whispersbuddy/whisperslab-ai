    Questions attorneys ask before they automate

    Want to talk it through?Book a free discovery call →
    What can a small law firm automate with AI?+
    A small law firm can automate the administrative work around its matters: acknowledging and routing new inquiries, preparing conflict searches, drafting engagement letters from approved templates, collecting client documents, sending status updates, and chasing unpaid invoices. Legal advice, conflict decisions, court deadlines, and trust accounting stay with attorneys and staff.
    Is it ethical for lawyers to use AI and automation?+
    It can be, when the firm meets its professional duties. In Formal Opinion 512, released July 29, 2024, the American Bar Association's ethics committee said lawyers using generative AI must consider their duties of competence, confidentiality, client communication, and reasonable fees. State bars publish their own guidance, so firms should also check the rules in their jurisdiction.
    How is client confidentiality protected when a law firm automates?+
    Confidentiality is protected through how the system is built. Information moves only between tools the firm already controls, each workflow gets only the access it needs, AI steps run through business accounts rather than consumer chat apps, every automated action is logged, and anything substantive waits for attorney approval. Prospective client information collected at intake gets the same care as client files.
    Does law firm automation work with Clio or MyCase?+
    It depends on the tool and the subscription plan. Most modern practice management, email, e-signature, and billing tools offer some way to connect other software, but the options vary. Whispers Lab confirms which connections are possible for a firm's specific tools during the $250 Automation Audit, before anything is built.
    How much does automation cost for a small law firm?+
    With Whispers Lab, it starts with a $250 Automation Audit that maps intake, conflicts, onboarding, and billing workflows and ranks them by time saved. If the firm goes ahead, the build is quoted as a fixed price with no hourly billing, and the $250 is credited toward it. Ongoing maintenance and new workflows are available through the AI Growth Partner plan at $500 a month.
    Will automation replace paralegals or legal assistants?+
    No. Automation removes the retyping, chasing, and copying between systems, not the judgment. Paralegals and legal assistants spend that time on work that needs a person, such as preparing matters, talking with clients, and checking the details an attorney relies on.
