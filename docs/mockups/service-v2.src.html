    Questions owners ask first

    Still not sure?Ask Haris on a free discovery call →
    What is document automation?+
    Document automation is software that handles documents without manual retyping. It reads incoming documents such as invoices, bank statements, and forms and files their data into your systems, and it creates outgoing documents such as reports and letters from templates. A well-built setup checks its own work and sends anything uncertain to a person for review.
    How much does document automation cost for a small business?+
    At Whispers Lab it starts with a $250 Automation Audit that measures which documents cost your team the most time. If you go ahead, the build is quoted as a fixed price for the specific workflows, with no hourly billing, and the $250 is credited toward it. Ongoing maintenance and new workflows are available through the AI Growth Partner plan at $500 a month.
    How long does it take to set up?+
    A document automation workflow built in the Whispers Lab Core Build is production-ready in under 30 days. That includes designing the data flow, building and connecting the automation, testing it against real sample documents, and training your team to review flagged items.
    Is it accurate enough for financial documents?+
    It is accurate enough when the system is built to be checked. The AI is told exactly which fields to return and in what format, plain rules confirm totals and dates, and any field that fails a check goes to a person instead of being filed. In the Financial Document Reader that Whispers Lab built, bank statement transactions are extracted in the same structure every time, which removed manual data entry entirely.
    Will it work with the software we already use?+
    In most cases, yes. Data can be written to accounting tools like Xero and QuickBooks, to Airtable or Google Sheets, to a CRM like HubSpot, or to your own database, and documents can be collected from email, shared folders, or forms. The Whispers Lab Automation Audit confirms the exact connections before anything is built.
    Does document automation replace admin staff?+
    No. Document automation removes the retyping, not the judgment. Staff who used to enter data spend that time reviewing flagged documents, fixing real exceptions, and handling work that needs a person, such as approving payments or answering a client's question about a number.
