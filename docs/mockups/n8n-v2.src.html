Clearing the air

Questions owners ask about n8n

Have a workflow in mind?Book a free discovery call →
What is n8n used for?+
n8n is a workflow automation platform used to connect business apps and run multi-step processes without manual work, such as logging emails to a CRM, routing new leads, generating documents, or sending alerts. Workflows can branch on conditions, run custom JavaScript, and call AI models where a step needs it.
Is n8n free?+
n8n has a free self-hosted Community edition that includes most features, but the business provides and maintains the server. n8n Cloud is a paid, fully managed service billed by workflow executions, where one execution is a single run of a whole workflow no matter how many steps it has. Current prices are listed on n8n's pricing page.
Should a small business use n8n Cloud or self-host n8n?+
n8n Cloud suits most small businesses because n8n handles hosting, updates, and scaling. Self-hosting suits businesses that need full control over where their data lives and have someone to handle updates, backups, and security. Whispers Lab recommends one during the Automation Audit based on the data involved, the budget, and who will maintain it.
Can n8n workflows use AI like OpenAI or Claude?+
Yes. An n8n workflow can send text or documents to an AI model to classify, extract, or summarize, then use the result in later steps. Whispers Lab adds AI only where a plain rule cannot do the job, because rules are cheaper and more predictable. Neither of Whispers Lab's two published n8n builds needed AI.
Who maintains n8n workflows after they are built?+
After a Whispers Lab build, the business receives documentation, video walkthroughs, and an error workflow that alerts a named person when a run fails. Businesses that want ongoing monitoring, fixes, and a new workflow added each month can use the AI Growth Partner plan at $500 a month.
How much does it cost to have n8n workflows built?+
Whispers Lab starts with a $250 Automation Audit that maps which workflows are worth building. Builds are quoted as a fixed price with no hourly billing, and the $250 is credited toward the build. n8n's own subscription or server costs are separate from the build and are estimated during the Audit.
