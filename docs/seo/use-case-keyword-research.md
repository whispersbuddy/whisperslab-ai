# Use-case hub keyword research (2026-10-18)

Source: Semrush Keyword Magic Tool via the brand-sem workspace, US database, volume per month, KD = keyword difficulty %. Only volumes and KD shown by the tool. Nothing estimated.
Free skill check: the open-source SEO skills found (Claude SEO, Corey Haines marketing skills, davila7 seo-optimizer) cover audits and briefs but give no search volume, so the Semrush data above is the volume source. Claude SEO is already installed here.

## Verdict
Demand exists at department level and at "how to automate X" level. It is mixed with software-vendor results, so the pages win only with proof (case studies) and specifics. Realistic targets for a young domain are KD 35 or lower, plus long-tail phrases under each department.

## Decisions
1. Build the hub `/use-cases` on "small business automation" (KD 31) and "workflow automation examples" (KD 34).
2. Department pages in priority order: finance and accounting, e-commerce and inventory, sales and leads, customer support, operations (onboarding, data entry, reporting), CRM and marketing. HR last (no proof).
3. Use the long-tail phrases as H2/FAQ questions on each page, since page-level head terms for sales, CRM and email are too competitive.
4. Do not write "best X software" content. Terms with C (commercial) intent such as "best marketing automation platform" are software comparisons and stay out.
5. Re-check Search Console after 4 to 6 weeks, then give the best-performing ideas their own pages.

Caveats: volumes are monthly US estimates from the tool. KD is Semrush's difficulty estimate, not a ranking promise. Several head terms (email, sales, CRM) are dominated by software vendors.
